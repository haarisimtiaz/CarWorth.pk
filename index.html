What this model is (and isn't) good at

Solid coverage

Common sedans and hatchbacks — Corolla, City, Civic, Alto, Cultus, Swift — trained on thousands of real resale listings across major cities.

Thinner coverage

Luxury/SUV segment (Land Cruiser, Prado, Fortuner, Sportage, Tucson, H6) is included but with far fewer real examples than mainstream cars — treat those estimates as rougher.

Not accounted for

Accident history, paint/body condition, negotiation, and seller urgency aren't in the data — two "identical" cars can genuinely differ by a meaningful margin.
